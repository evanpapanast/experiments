    About this report

    This is one of my experiments: I wanted to see how quickly I could take a public dataset from source to a Power BI-style report using a cloud data warehouse, SQL and an AI assistant (Claude), with everything versioned on GitHub.

    Eurostat API→ Python: extract & tidy→ Google BigQuery (star schema)→ SQL reporting views→ Data quality gate (22 checks)→ Interactive HTML report

    Definitions

    • Uses AI: the enterprise uses at least one of eight AI technologies (seven before 2025) (text mining, speech recognition, generating text/speech/code, image recognition, machine learning, workflow automation, autonomous machines, generating images/video/audio).
    • Population: enterprises with 10 or more employees in industry and non-financial services (NACE C–N excluding K, plus 95.1). Small = 10–49, medium = 50–249, large = 250+ employees.
    • Uses and how AI was sourced are shown as a share of enterprises that use AI. Barriers are shown as a share of enterprises that considered AI but do not use it.
    • The survey was not run on AI in 2022. Some questions change between waves, so a few indicators exist only for some years.
    • Values flagged by Eurostat as low reliability (u) or with a break in series (b) are marked in tooltips and tables.
